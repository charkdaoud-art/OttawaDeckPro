Picking a railing is one of the biggest choices on a new deck. It frames the view, sets the style of the whole backyard and makes up a real share of the budget. When homeowners compare **glass vs aluminum railing in Ottawa**, it usually comes down to three things: price, look and maintenance.

Both are low-maintenance. Both pair well with composite and PVC decking. But they are not the same, and the right answer depends on your view, your budget and how much cleaning you are willing to do. This guide compares the two side by side so you can decide with real numbers.

## Glass vs aluminum railing cost in Ottawa

Railing is priced by the **linear foot**, measured along the open edges of the deck. Here are our installed price ranges in Ottawa, before tax:

- **Aluminum railing:** about $90 to $140 per linear foot
- **Glass railing:** about $180 to $250 per linear foot
- **Wood railing (for reference):** about $60 to $80 per linear foot

In short, glass costs about twice as much as aluminum per foot.

> Glass railing runs about $180 to $250 per linear foot installed in Ottawa, and aluminum about $90 to $140. Put glass where the view matters and aluminum everywhere else to get the look for less.

- **All aluminum:** about $3,240 to $5,040

## How glass and aluminum railings look

### Glass: the view comes first

Glass railing puts clear panels between the posts, so you look through the railing instead of at it. It is the premium choice when the backyard looks out on something worth seeing: a ravine, the water, mature trees, open green space or a fairway. On a second-storey deck, it keeps the space feeling open and bright.

Glass also comes in more than one style. Clear panels give the widest view. Frosted panels add privacy while still letting light through, which works well on decks that face a neighbour.

### Aluminum: clean lines and more styles

Aluminum railing uses pickets or balusters between a top and bottom rail. Black gives a sharp, modern line. White suits more traditional homes. You can also choose horizontal privacy screens or decorative panels for sections that need more coverage.

Aluminum is a strong choice when there is no big view to protect, when you want some privacy, or when the railing needs to match other metal work on the house.

## Maintenance through Ottawa winters

Neither material needs staining, sealing or painting. That is the main reason both are so popular on composite and PVC decks. The difference is in the cleaning.

### Glass railing upkeep

- **Cleaning shows.** Pollen, rain spots, dust and fingerprints are easy to see on clear glass. Plan to wash the panels a few times a season if you want them spotless.
- **Gentle products only.** Use a soft cloth or squeegee with mild soap and water. Avoid anything abrasive that could scratch the glass or the frame finish.
- **Frosted panels hide more.** They show spots less than clear glass.

### Aluminum railing upkeep

- **Very little work.** The finish is applied at the factory, so there is nothing to repaint.
- **A wash once or twice a season** with soap and water keeps it clean.
- **No rot or splinters.** Freeze and thaw cycles do not split it the way they can split wood.

If you want the lowest possible upkeep, aluminum wins. If you are happy to clean glass in exchange for the view, glass is worth it.

## Code basics for glass and aluminum railings

In code terms, a deck railing is called a **guard**. Both glass and aluminum guards must meet the Ontario Building Code, and the City of Ottawa checks guards during inspections when a deck needs a permit. The general rules for houses are:

- A guard is needed when the deck walking surface is more than 600 mm (about 24 inches) above the ground next to it.
- The guard must be at least 900 mm (about 35 inches) high when the deck is up to 1.8 m above the ground, and 1070 mm (about 42 inches) when it is higher.
- Openings must be small enough to stop a 100 mm (about 4 inch) sphere.

### Extra rules for glass

Glass has a few more requirements. The City of Ottawa's advisory on glass guards says glass used in a guard must be safety glass, such as tempered or laminated glass that meets the referenced CAN/CGSB or ANSI standards. The same advisory says guards that contain glass are required to be designed by a Professional Engineer, and that permit drawings must show the glass type and thickness, connections and anchorage.

Rules change and every site is different, so confirm the details for your deck with the City of Ottawa. Our guide on [deck permits in Ottawa](/post/deck-permit-ottawa) explains when a permit is required.

## Mixing glass and aluminum railing

You do not have to pick only one. Many Ottawa homeowners mix them, and it is often the smartest way to spend the railing budget.

- **Glass on the view side.** Put glass where you sit and look out.
- **Aluminum on the sides and stairs.** These sections are seen less and cost about half as much.
- **Matching frames.** Black-framed glass next to black aluminum pickets reads as one design.

This approach keeps the open view where it matters and saves money where it does not. We show the mix in your 3D design so you can see it before anything is built.

## Glass vs aluminum railing: which should you choose?

Here is a quick way to decide.

**Choose glass if:**
- Your yard looks out on water, trees, a ravine or open space
- You have a second-storey deck and want it to feel open
- You do not mind cleaning the panels a few times a season
- The railing budget has room for about $180 to $250 per linear foot

**Choose aluminum if:**
- You want the lowest upkeep possible
- You want privacy or there is no big view to protect
- You want a clean modern or traditional look for less
- You prefer a budget of about $90 to $140 per linear foot

**Mix them if** you want the view without paying glass prices on every edge.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is glass railing more expensive than aluminum in Ottawa?
Yes. Glass runs about $180 to $250 per linear foot installed and aluminum about $90 to $140, before tax. Glass costs about twice as much per foot.

### Is glass railing hard to maintain?
It needs no staining or painting, but clear glass shows pollen, rain spots and fingerprints. Most owners wash it a few times a season with mild soap and water.

### Can you mix glass and aluminum railing on one deck?
Yes. A common setup is glass on the view side and aluminum on the sides and stairs. It keeps the view open and lowers the total cost.

### Does glass railing need a permit or an engineer in Ottawa?
If your deck needs a permit, the guards are part of it. The City of Ottawa's advisory says guards with glass must use safety glass and be designed by a Professional Engineer. Confirm the details for your project with the City.

### Which railing is best for a composite deck?
Both pair well with composite because neither needs staining. Choose glass for a view and aluminum for the lowest upkeep and cost.
