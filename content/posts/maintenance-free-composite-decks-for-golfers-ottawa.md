## The real cost of a "cheap" wood deck

Pressure-treated wood is the lowest price to build. It is a great choice for many backyards. But wood asks for your time every year.

- **Cleaning every spring** to remove mildew and grime after an Ottawa winter.
- **Sanding and re-staining** every couple of years to stop greying, cracking and splinters.
- **Board and fastener repairs** as boards cup, twist and loosen through freeze and thaw.

Each of those jobs lands in spring and summer. That is exactly when the course is calling. Hire it out and the cost adds up fast. Do it yourself and you give up weekends.

> Composite and PVC decks never need staining or sealing. A wash with soap and water once or twice a season keeps them looking new.

## Composite vs PVC: which is better for a golfer?

Both are true maintenance-free surfaces. The difference is in the details.

**Composite decking** blends wood fibres and plastic under a protective outer shell. It feels solid underfoot, comes in rich wood-tone colours, and is the most popular choice for Ottawa homes. Many leading composite brands back their boards with warranties of 25 years or longer.

**PVC decking** contains no wood at all. It is fully waterproof, lighter, and the premium choice for decks near pools, hot tubs and anywhere that stays damp.

For most golfers, composite hits the sweet spot of looks, durability and price. If your deck wraps around a pool or hot tub, PVC is worth the upgrade.

## Design ideas golfers love

A maintenance-free deck is the starting point. The fun is in making it yours.

### A post-round lounge

Think of the deck as your own 19th hole. A covered or partly covered lounge area with deep seating, an outdoor TV for weekend tournaments and a bar ledge turns a deck into the place everyone wants to end the day.

![Luxury composite deck with covered patio and lounge seating in Ottawa](luxury-composite-deck-covered-patio-ottawa.webp "Covered lounge space on a composite deck.")

### Room for a putting green

Many golfers plan the deck and the yard together. A ground-level deck can step down onto an artificial putting green, so you can practise your short game with a drink in hand. We plan the deck layout, stairs and transitions so the green fits naturally. Ask us about it at your consultation.

### Lighting for evening rounds of a different kind

Stair riser lights, post cap lights and soft perimeter lighting make the deck usable long after sunset. They also make stairs safer when you are carrying a tray of drinks.

![Composite stairs with LED step lights and black aluminum railing in Ottawa](composite-stairs-led-lights-black-aluminum-railing-ottawa.webp "LED stair lights with black aluminum railing.")

### Smart storage for clubs and gear

On a raised deck, the space underneath can be enclosed with skirting and a door. It becomes a dry spot for golf bags, pull carts and patio cushions, right where you need them.

## What does a maintenance-free deck cost in Ottawa?

Here are typical installed price ranges in Ottawa, before tax:

- **Composite decking:** about $65 to $100+ per square foot
- **PVC decking:** about $85 to $120+ per square foot
- **Aluminum railing:** about $90 to $140 per linear foot
- **Glass railing:** about $180 to $250 per linear foot

Height, stairs, site access, permits and design details all change the final number. For a quick idea of your own project, try our [deck cost calculator](/decks#calculator).

The upfront cost is higher than pressure-treated wood. Over the life of the deck, you skip the staining, sealing and most of the repairs. For a golfer, the real return is the time you get back.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a composite deck really maintenance-free?
Composite never needs staining, sealing or sanding. Plan on washing it with soap and water once or twice a season to remove pollen, dirt and leaves. That is it.

### Can you build a deck next to a putting green?
Yes. A ground-level deck can step down onto an artificial putting green. We plan the deck height, stairs and edges so the transition looks clean and works well.

### How much does a composite deck cost in Ottawa?
Composite decks in Ottawa typically run about $65 to $100+ per square foot installed, before tax. Railings, stairs, height and design details change the total. Our [deck cost calculator](/decks#calculator) gives you a quick range.

### Is PVC better than composite?
PVC is fully waterproof and contains no wood, so it is the premium choice around pools and hot tubs. Composite costs less, offers rich wood-tone colours and is the most popular choice for Ottawa homes.
