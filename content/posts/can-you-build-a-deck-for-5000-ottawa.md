"Can you build a deck for $5,000?" pops up on Google right under searches for deck builders in Ottawa. It is a fair question. Five thousand dollars is real money, and you want to know what it gets you before you start calling builders in Ottawa.

The honest answer: **yes, a small, ground-level pressure-treated deck can come in around $5,000 before tax in Ottawa.** Think about 100 to 165 square feet, low to the ground, with a few steps and no railing. A raised deck, a railing, a full set of stairs or composite boards will usually push the price past $5,000. This guide shows exactly what $5,000 buys, using our real installed prices.

## What a deck for $5,000 buys in each material

Here is roughly how much deck surface $5,000 covers at our typical installed prices in Ottawa, before tax. This is the deck alone, with no railing or stairs:

- **Pressure-treated** ($30 to $45 per sq ft): about **110 to 165 sq ft**
- **Cedar** ($50 to $70 per sq ft): about **70 to 100 sq ft**
- **Composite** ($65 to $100+ per sq ft): about **50 to 75 sq ft**
- **PVC** ($85 to $120+ per sq ft): about **40 to 58 sq ft**

For context, a 10x12 deck is 120 square feet. So in pressure-treated wood, $5,000 gets you a useful small deck. In composite, it gets you a landing about the size of a large rug.

## What pushes a small deck past $5,000

### Height and railing

Ontario's Building Code generally requires a guard (railing) when a deck's walking surface is more than 600 mm (24 in.) above the ground. Once a deck needs a railing, the budget changes fast.

Take a raised 10x10 pressure-treated deck attached to the house. The three open sides need about 26 linear feet of railing after a 4 ft stair opening:

- Deck: 100 sq ft = **$3,000 to $4,500**
- Aluminum railing, 26 linear ft at $90 to $140 = **$2,340 to $3,640**
- Full flight of stairs: **$2,500 to $5,500**
- **Rough total: about $7,840 to $13,640 before tax**

Same size deck, but height alone takes it well past $5,000.

### Stairs

A few steps cost about $800 to $1,800. A full flight costs about $2,500 to $5,500. On a small budget, stairs can be a third of the total.

### Permits

In Ottawa, an attached deck more than 600 mm (24 in.) above the ground needs a building permit, no matter how small it is. Read our [deck permit guide](/post/deck-permit-ottawa). A ground-level deck usually avoids this.

### Old deck removal and extras

Taking down an old deck, lighting, skirting and privacy screens are all priced on top.

## How to get the most deck for your budget

- **Stay low.** A ground-level deck avoids railings, a full flight of stairs and usually a permit.
- **Keep the shape simple.** A plain rectangle with boards running one way is the most efficient to build.
- **Choose pressure-treated.** It gives you the most square feet per dollar. Plan to [stain it](/staining) every few years.
- **Add extras later.** Lighting, planters and privacy screens can often come in a second phase.
- **Size it for real use.** A 10x10 deck fits a small bistro set and a BBQ. If you want a full dining table, plan for more.
