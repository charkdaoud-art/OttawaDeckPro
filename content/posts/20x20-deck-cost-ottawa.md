A 20x20 deck is a big outdoor room. At 400 square feet, it fits a dining table, a lounge set and a BBQ zone with space to spare. So it is no surprise that **20x20 deck cost** is one of the most common questions Ottawa homeowners ask Google before they call a builder.

The short answer: a 20x20 deck in Ottawa typically costs about **$12,000 to $18,000 in pressure-treated wood** and about **$26,000 to $40,000+ in composite**, installed and before tax. Railings and stairs are extra. This guide shows every number, explains what moves the price, and walks through real sample budgets.

## 20x20 deck cost in Ottawa by material

A 20x20 deck is 400 square feet. Here is what the deck itself costs at our typical installed prices in Ottawa, before tax:

- **Pressure-treated** ($30 to $45 per sq ft): about **$12,000 to $18,000**
- **Cedar** ($50 to $70 per sq ft): about **$20,000 to $28,000**
- **Composite** ($65 to $100+ per sq ft): about **$26,000 to $40,000+**
- **PVC** ($85 to $120+ per sq ft): about **$34,000 to $48,000+**

These numbers cover the structure and the decking surface: footings, framing, boards and labour. Railings and stairs are priced separately, because they change so much from one yard to the next.

## Railings and stairs for a 20x20 deck

### Railing cost

If your deck is attached to the house along one 20 ft side, the other three sides are open: 20 + 20 + 20 = 60 linear feet. Leave a 4 ft opening for the stairs and you need about **56 linear feet of railing**.

- **Wood railing** ($60 to $80 per linear ft): about $3,360 to $4,480
- **Aluminum railing** ($90 to $140 per linear ft): about $5,040 to $7,840
- **Glass railing** ($180 to $250 per linear ft): about $10,080 to $14,000

On a 20x20 deck, railing is a big line item. Glass can cost more than the entire surface of a pressure-treated deck. Compare styles on our [railings page](/railings).

### Do you need a railing at all?

Ontario's Building Code generally requires a guard (railing) when a deck's walking surface is more than 600 mm (24 in.) above the ground. A low, ground-level 20x20 deck may not need one. A raised deck does. Always confirm your own project with the City of Ottawa.

### Stair cost

- **1 to 4 steps** (typical for a low deck): about $800 to $1,800
- **Full flight** (typical for a raised deck): about $2,500 to $5,500

Wide feature stairs that run along a full side of the deck take more material and labour, so plan toward the top of the range.

## What changes the price of a 20x20 deck

### Height

A 400 sq ft deck at ground level needs short posts and simple framing. Lift that same deck to a second-storey door and you need taller posts, larger beams, bracing, a full flight of stairs and a railing on every open side. Height is the single biggest swing factor.

### Permits

Height also decides whether you need a building permit. In Ottawa, an attached deck more than 600 mm (24 in.) above the ground needs one at any size. Our [deck permit guide](/post/deck-permit-ottawa) explains the rules in plain English.

### Shape and design

A simple square is the most efficient shape to build. A 20x20 footprint can also be split into two levels, wrapped with a picture-frame border, or broken up with angled corners. Each detail adds material and labour.

### Extras

On a deck this size, people often add lighting, privacy screens, skirting or a pergola. Each one is priced on its own.

## Is a 20x20 deck the right size for you?

A 20x20 deck gives you room for separate zones: dining, lounging and cooking. It suits larger yards and families who host often.

- **For a table for 6 to 8 plus a lounge set**, 20x20 is comfortable.
- **If your yard is narrow**, a long, shallower deck can give you the same space with better flow.
- **If budget is tight**, a smaller deck in composite can cost about the same as a 20x20 in pressure-treated, with far less upkeep.
