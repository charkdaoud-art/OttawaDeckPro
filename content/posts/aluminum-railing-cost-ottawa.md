If you are pricing a new deck or replacing an old wood railing, the first question is simple: what is the **aluminum railing cost in Ottawa**? In 2026, our installed price for aluminum railing is about **$90 to $140 per linear foot**, before tax.

That range is wide for a reason. Height, stairs, layout and the style you choose all move the number. This guide shows how railing is priced, gives worked examples for common deck sizes, compares aluminum with wood and glass, and covers the code basics every Ottawa homeowner should know.

## What does aluminum railing cost in Ottawa?

Railing is priced by the **linear foot**. That is the length of railing measured along the edge of the deck, not the area of the deck.

Here are our installed price ranges in Ottawa, before tax:

- **Wood railing:** about $60 to $80 per linear foot
- **Aluminum railing:** about $90 to $140 per linear foot
- **Glass railing:** about $180 to $250 per linear foot

Aluminum sits in the middle. It costs more than wood up front, and far less than glass. For many Ottawa homeowners, that is the sweet spot: a clean, modern look with almost no upkeep.

## How to estimate your aluminum railing cost

You can get a rough number in about two minutes.

- **Measure the open edges.** Walk the deck and add up every edge that needs a railing. The side against the house usually does not.
- **Subtract the stair openings.** Where the stairs come off the deck, there is a gap in the railing.
- **Add the stair rails.** If your stairs need rails, add their length too. Stair runs are measured on site, since the slope adds length.
- **Multiply by the price range.** Use $90 at the low end and $140 at the high end.

## What changes the price of aluminum railing

Two decks with the same linear footage can land at opposite ends of the range. Here is why.

### Height of the deck

A low deck and a second-storey deck need different guard heights under the Ontario Building Code (more on that below). Taller railing uses more material. A high deck can also be harder to reach and install safely, which adds labour.

### Stairs

Railing on stairs takes more cutting and fitting than a straight run. A deck with one short set of steps costs less to rail than a deck with a full flight down to the yard. Stairs themselves are priced separately: about $800 to $1,800 for 1 to 4 steps, and about $2,500 to $5,500 for a full flight.

### Layout and corners

Long straight runs are the most efficient. Every corner, angle and change in direction adds posts and fitting time. Wraparound decks, angled corners and multi-level decks push the price up.

### Style and add-ons

The basic picket style is the most common. Upgrades like horizontal privacy screens, glass inserts in sections, post cap lights or a drink rail on top move the price toward the higher end, or beyond it for glass sections. If you want glass panels, they are priced as glass railing.

![Elevated composite deck with a horizontal aluminum privacy screen and black railing in Ottawa](elevated-composite-deck-horizontal-privacy-screen-ottawa.webp "A horizontal aluminum privacy screen paired with black railing.")

### New deck vs existing deck

On a new deck, we plan the posts and blocking into the framing from day one. Adding aluminum railing to an older deck can take more work if the framing needs reinforcing where the posts mount. We check this at your estimate.

### When wood makes sense

Wood is the lowest upfront cost. It suits pressure-treated and cedar decks where you want a matching look. The trade-off is upkeep. A wood railing needs staining or sealing on a schedule to stay sound and good-looking through Ottawa winters. See our [deck staining](/staining) page if you already have one.

### When aluminum makes sense

Aluminum never needs staining or painting, and it does not rot or splinter. It pairs well with composite and PVC decking, so the whole deck stays low-maintenance. Black gives a clean, modern line. White suits more traditional homes.

### When glass makes sense

Glass is the premium choice when the view is the point: a ravine, the water, open green space or a fairway behind the house. It costs about twice as much as aluminum per linear foot. Many homeowners mix the two, with glass on the view side and aluminum on the sides and stairs.

## Ontario code basics for deck railings

In code terms, a deck railing is called a **guard**. The Ontario Building Code sets the rules, and the City of Ottawa checks guards during inspections. Here are the basics, based on Subsection 9.8.8 of the Code for houses:

- **When a guard is needed:** when the deck walking surface is more than 600 mm (about 24 inches) above the ground next to it.
- **Minimum height:** 900 mm (about 35 inches) when the deck is up to 1.8 m above the ground, and 1070 mm (about 42 inches) when it is higher than 1.8 m.
- **Openings:** gaps between pickets and under the bottom rail must be small enough to stop a 100 mm (about 4 inch) sphere.
- **Stairs:** exterior stairs with more than 3 risers need a handrail.

Rules can change and every site is different, so confirm the details for your deck with the City of Ottawa. If your deck needs a permit, the City inspects the guards at both the framing and final inspections. Our guide on [deck permits in Ottawa](/post/deck-permit-ottawa) explains when one is required.

## Is aluminum railing worth the cost in Ottawa?

For most homeowners, yes, especially on a composite or PVC deck. Here is the case for it:

- **No staining or painting.** The finish is applied at the factory.
- **No rot or splinters.** Freeze and thaw cycles do not split it the way they can split wood.
- **Easy care.** A wash with soap and water keeps it clean.
- **Matches low-maintenance decking.** A composite deck with a wood railing still has a wood railing to maintain.

If budget is tight, wood railing on a wood deck is a fair choice. If you want the deck to take care of itself, aluminum is where most of the long-term savings in time come from.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does aluminum railing cost per foot in Ottawa?
Our installed price for aluminum railing in Ottawa is about $90 to $140 per linear foot, before tax. Height, stairs, corners and style set where your project lands in that range.

### How much does aluminum railing cost for a 12 x 16 deck?
With railing on three sides and a 4-foot stair opening, a 12 x 16 deck needs about 36 linear feet. At $90 to $140 per linear foot, that is about $3,240 to $5,040 installed, before tax.

### Is aluminum railing cheaper than glass?
Yes. Aluminum runs about $90 to $140 per linear foot and glass about $180 to $250, installed, before tax. Many homeowners use glass only on the view side to balance cost and look.

### How high does a deck railing need to be in Ontario?
Under the Ontario Building Code, a guard is generally needed when the deck is more than 600 mm above the ground. It must be at least 900 mm high, or 1070 mm if the deck is more than 1.8 m above the ground. Confirm the details for your deck with the City of Ottawa.

### Can you add aluminum railing to an existing deck?
Yes, as long as the framing can hold the posts securely. We check the framing at your estimate and tell you if any reinforcing is needed.

### Does aluminum railing need maintenance?
Very little. It never needs staining or painting. A wash with soap and water once or twice a season keeps it looking clean.
