A 14x20 deck is one of the most popular sizes we see in Ottawa. At 280 square feet, it is big enough for a dining table and a lounge set, but it still fits most suburban yards in Kanata, Barrhaven and Orleans. That is why **14x20 deck cost** is such a common search.

Here is the short answer: a 14x20 deck in Ottawa typically costs about **$8,400 to $12,600 in pressure-treated wood** and about **$18,200 to $28,000+ in composite**, installed and before tax. Railings and stairs are extra. Below we break down every number, including a detail most cost guides miss: which way the deck faces the house changes how much railing you need.

## 14x20 deck cost in Ottawa by material

A 14x20 deck is 280 square feet. At our typical installed prices in Ottawa, before tax, the deck itself costs:

- **Pressure-treated** ($30 to $45 per sq ft): about **$8,400 to $12,600**
- **Cedar** ($50 to $70 per sq ft): about **$14,000 to $19,600**
- **Composite** ($65 to $100+ per sq ft): about **$18,200 to $28,000+**
- **PVC** ($85 to $120+ per sq ft): about **$23,800 to $33,600+**

That covers footings, framing, decking and labour. Railings, stairs and extras like lighting are priced on top.

> A 14x20 deck in Ottawa costs about $8,400 to $33,600+ for the deck alone. Railing can add $2,640 to $12,500 more, depending on style and layout.

## How much railing does a 14x20 deck need?

This is where layout matters. A raised deck attached to the house has three open sides. Which side touches the house changes the total.

### Layout 1: the 20 ft side against the house

The open sides are 14 + 20 + 14 = 48 linear feet. Leave a 4 ft stair opening and you need about **44 linear feet** of railing.

- **Wood** ($60 to $80 per linear ft): about $2,640 to $3,520
- **Aluminum** ($90 to $140 per linear ft): about $3,960 to $6,160
- **Glass** ($180 to $250 per linear ft): about $7,920 to $11,000

### Layout 2: the 14 ft side against the house

The open sides are 20 + 14 + 20 = 54 linear feet. With a 4 ft stair opening, you need about **50 linear feet** of railing.

- **Wood:** about $3,000 to $4,000
- **Aluminum:** about $4,500 to $7,000
- **Glass:** about $9,000 to $12,500

Same deck, same square footage, but the second layout needs 6 more feet of railing. With glass, that difference alone can be over $1,000. Layout 1 also keeps more of the deck close to the house, which is handy for a BBQ near the kitchen door.

### When you need a railing

Ontario's Building Code generally requires a guard (railing) when a deck's walking surface is more than 600 mm (24 in.) above the ground. A low 14x20 deck may not need one. Confirm your own project with the City of Ottawa. Compare railing styles on our [railings page](/railings).

## Aluminum vs glass railing on a 14x20 deck

On a deck this size, the railing choice changes both the look and the budget.

- **Aluminum** gives a clean line and almost no upkeep. It is a popular pairing with composite decking.
- **Glass** keeps the view open. It suits yards that back onto green space, a ravine or water. It costs about twice as much per linear foot.
- **Mix them.** Some homeowners put glass on the side with the view and aluminum on the others to balance cost and look.

## Stairs for a 14x20 deck

- **1 to 4 steps** (a low deck): about $800 to $1,800
- **Full flight** (a raised deck): about $2,500 to $5,500

Where the stairs go matters too. Stairs that land on a patio or path are simpler than stairs that need a landing or turn.

## What else changes the cost of a 14x20 deck

- **Height.** Taller decks need longer posts, bigger beams, bracing and a full flight of stairs.
- **Permit.** In Ottawa, an attached deck more than 600 mm (24 in.) above the ground needs a building permit at any size. See our [deck permit guide](/post/deck-permit-ottawa).
- **Board line.** Premium composite boards cost more than standard lines. Read more in our guide to [composite deck cost per square foot](/post/composite-deck-cost-per-square-foot-ottawa).
- **Design details.** Picture-frame borders, two levels, diagonal boards and built-in benches all add material and labour.
- **Extras.** Lighting, privacy screens and skirting are priced on their own.

A 14x20 deck fits a dining table for 6 and a small lounge set. If you want a separate cooking zone or a hot tub, you may want more depth. If your yard is small, 14x20 can still work at ground level with a simple shape.
