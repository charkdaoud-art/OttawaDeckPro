## Step 1: the site visit and the "before" photo

Every estimate starts in your backyard. We measure the space, look at how the deck meets the house, check the slope, the trees and the access for materials, and listen to how you want to use the yard.

![Old dark-painted wood deck with wood railing and stairs before replacement in Ottawa](old-wood-deck-before-estimate-ottawa.webp "The deck today: painted wood, worn railings and stairs, and a big tree to work around.")

On this project, the existing deck is painted wood with wood railings and two sets of stairs. The paint is wearing off the boards and stair treads, and a large mature tree sits right beside the deck. Those details shape the design. The new deck has to keep the stairs where people walk, give the tree room, and line up with the patio stones.

### What to have ready for the visit

- **Your wish list.** Size, BBQ spot, seating, stairs, lighting, privacy.
- **Your budget range.** It helps us suggest the right materials from the start.
- **Your survey, if you have it.** It shows property lines, which matter for setbacks and permits.
- **Photos you like.** Our [gallery](/gallery) is a good place to start.

## Step 2: your 3D design

After the visit, we build your deck in 3D. You see the layout, the decking colour, the railings, the stairs and the lighting on your own house, before anything is built.

![3D design of a grey deck with black aluminum railing, skirting and lit stairs in Ottawa](3d-deck-design-black-aluminum-railing-ottawa.webp "The 3D design: grey decking, black aluminum railing, full skirting and lit stairs.")

For this home, the design swaps the painted wood for grey decking with matching skirting, black aluminum railing and step lights on the stairs. It keeps the front and side stairs, so the deck still flows to the patio and the side path.

### Why a 3D design matters

- **No guessing.** You see the finished look, not just a list of materials.
- **Easy changes.** Wider stairs, a different railing, another colour. We revise the design until it is right.
- **Better decisions.** Seeing glass next to aluminum, or two decking colours side by side, makes choosing much easier. Explore our [railings](/railings) and [deck materials](/decks).

This is a design, not a photo. The deck you approve is the deck our own crew builds.

## Step 3: your 2D build plan

The 3D design shows what the deck will look like. The 2D plan shows how it will be built.

![2D deck construction plan with layout, framing, section and helical pile details](2d-deck-construction-plan-helical-piles-ottawa.webp "The build plan: layout, framing, a cross section and the pile detail.")

For this project, the plan includes:

- **Deck size:** 24 ft wide by 12.5 ft deep, about 300 sq ft.
- **Support:** a 3-ply 2x8 pressure-treated beam, 11 ft from the house, on 3 helical piles (steel screw piles).
- **Joists:** 2x8 pressure-treated at 12 in. on centre.
- **Railing:** black aluminum, 36 in. high.
- **Stairs:** 4 steps, 8 ft wide at the front, and 5 steps, 4 ft wide on the side.

A plan like this does two jobs. It lets you compare quotes on the same terms, because you can see exactly what is in the price. And if your deck needs a building permit, drawings like these are a big part of the application. Read our guide: [Do I need a permit to build a deck in Ottawa?](/post/deck-permit-ottawa)

## Step 4: a clear, written estimate

With the design and plan agreed, you get a written estimate. It is based on what you approved, so the price matches the plan.

### What changes the price of a deck

- **Size and height.** Bigger and higher decks need more framing and support.
- **Materials.** Pressure-treated, cedar, composite or PVC.
- **Railings and stairs.** These add up fast on a raised deck.
- **Foundation.** Helical piles, concrete footings or a floating base.
- **Extras.** Lighting, skirting, privacy screens, demolition of the old deck.
- **Permits and site access.** City fees and tight yards add cost.

Here are our typical installed price ranges in Ottawa, before tax:

- **Pressure-treated:** about $30 to $45 per square foot
- **Cedar:** about $50 to $70 per square foot
- **Composite:** about $65 to $100+ per square foot
- **PVC:** about $85 to $120+ per square foot
- **Aluminum railing:** about $90 to $140 per linear foot
- **Glass railing:** about $180 to $250 per linear foot
- **Stairs:** about $800 to $1,800 for 1 to 4 steps, and $2,500 to $5,500 for a full flight

## How to compare deck estimates

If you are getting more than one deck estimate in Ottawa, check that each one covers the same things:

- **The same size, height and layout.** A plan makes this easy.
- **The same materials,** down to the decking line and railing type.
- **Foundation type** and how many piles or footings.
- **Demolition and disposal** of the old deck.
- **Permits:** who prepares the drawings and applies.
- **Insurance and warranty:** ask for proof of WSIB coverage and liability insurance, and what the workmanship warranty covers.

The cheapest number is not always the cheapest deck. Make sure you are comparing the same project.
