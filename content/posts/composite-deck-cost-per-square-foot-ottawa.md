If you are pricing a new deck, one number comes up first: **composite deck cost per square foot in Ottawa.** In 2026, a composite deck we build typically runs about **$65 to $100+ per square foot installed, before tax.** That covers the frame, the footings, the decking and the labour.

## Composite deck cost per square foot in Ottawa: the short answer

Here are our typical installed prices in Ottawa, before tax. They are the same ranges our [deck cost calculator](/decks#calculator) uses.

- **Pressure-treated:** about $30 to $45 per square foot
- **Cedar:** about $50 to $70 per square foot
- **Composite:** about $65 to $100+ per square foot
- **PVC:** about $85 to $120+ per square foot

Railings and stairs are priced on their own, so they are not inside the per square foot number:

- **Wood railing:** about $60 to $80 per linear foot
- **Aluminum railing:** about $90 to $140 per linear foot
- **Glass railing:** about $180 to $250 per linear foot
- **Stairs, 1 to 4 steps:** about $800 to $1,800
- **Full flight of stairs:** about $2,500 to $5,500

> For most Ottawa composite decks, plan on $65 to $100+ per square foot for the deck itself, then add railings and stairs on top. That gives you a realistic starting budget.

## What is included in the per square foot price

When we quote a composite deck by the square foot, the number covers the whole structure, not just the boards.

- **Footings** to carry the deck.
- **Framing:** beams, joists and hardware sized for the deck and the boards.
- **Composite decking** and fasteners, installed to the manufacturer's guide.
- **Labour** from our own crew.

Items like railings, stairs, skirting, lighting, privacy screens and removal of an old deck are priced on top, so you can see what drives the total.

## What raises or lowers composite deck cost per square foot

### Deck height

A ground-level deck needs short posts and simple framing. A raised or second-storey deck needs taller posts, bigger beams, more bracing and usually a full flight of stairs. Height is one of the biggest reasons a deck moves toward the top of the range.

Height also decides whether you need a permit. In Ottawa, an attached deck more than 600 mm (24 in.) above the ground needs a building permit. Our [deck permit guide](/post/deck-permit-ottawa) explains the rules in plain English. Always confirm your own project with the City.

### Board line and colour

Composite brands sell several lines, from entry boards to premium boards with deeper wood grain, richer colours and stronger surface shells. Premium boards cost more per board. Many leading composite brands back their boards with warranties of 25 years or longer, and the length often changes by line, so check the warranty on the exact board you choose.

### Layout and pattern

A straight, rectangular deck with boards running one way is the most efficient to build. Diagonal boards, herringbone patterns and inlays look great but use more material and more cutting. They can also need joists closer together. Trex, for example, says joists for standard decking should generally be no more than 16 inches apart, and that 12-inch spacing is required when boards run diagonally. More joists means more framing in the price.

### Picture-frame border and multiple levels

A picture-frame border wraps the deck edge in boards that run the other way. It hides cut board ends and gives a finished, custom look. Two-level decks and wraparound stairs add even more detail work. These features are popular on higher-end projects and raise the per square foot cost.

![Two-level grey composite deck with stair riser lights and white railing in Ottawa](two-level-grey-composite-deck-step-lights-ottawa.webp "A two-level composite deck with riser lights and white railing.")

### Site access and old deck removal

A yard with a wide gate and flat ground is faster to work in. Tight side yards, steep slopes, or a deck that sits over a walkout take more time. If an old deck needs to come down first, removal and disposal are added to the quote.

## Composite vs wood: cost over time

Composite costs more upfront than pressure-treated or cedar. A pressure-treated deck can cost less than half as much per square foot to build. The difference is what happens after.

- **Wood** needs cleaning, sanding and re-staining every few years to stay protected. You either give up weekends or pay for [deck staining](/staining).
- **Composite** never needs staining or sealing. A wash with soap and water once or twice a season keeps it looking good.

If you plan to stay in your home for years, the time and upkeep you skip is part of the value. For a closer look at the materials, read our guide to [composite vs wood decking](/post/composite-vs-wood-decking-for-ottawa-homeowners-which-is-the-right-choice).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a composite deck cost per square foot in Ottawa?
Our composite decks typically run about $65 to $100+ per square foot installed, before tax. Railings and stairs are priced separately.

### Does the per square foot price include railings and stairs?
No. Railings are priced per linear foot and stairs are priced as a flat amount. We list them separately so you can compare options.

### Why do some composite decks cost more per square foot?
Height, board line, diagonal or herringbone layouts, picture-frame borders, multiple levels and tight site access all add material or labour. Each one pushes the price toward the top of the range.

### Is composite worth the extra cost over pressure-treated?
Composite costs more to build but never needs staining or sealing. If you want to skip yearly upkeep and plan to stay in your home, many homeowners find it worth it.

### Do I need a permit for a composite deck in Ottawa?
The material does not matter, the height and design do. An attached deck more than 600 mm (24 in.) above the ground needs a permit. Confirm your project with the City of Ottawa before you build.
