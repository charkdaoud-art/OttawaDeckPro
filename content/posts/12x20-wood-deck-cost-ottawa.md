A 12x20 deck is a practical, family-sized deck. At 240 square feet, it fits a table for 6 and a couple of lounge chairs without taking over the yard. Built in wood, it is also one of the most affordable ways to add real outdoor living space. That is why **12x20 wood deck cost** is such a common question.

The short answer: a 12x20 wood deck in Ottawa typically costs about **$7,200 to $10,800 in pressure-treated wood** or about **$12,000 to $16,800 in cedar**, installed and before tax. Railings and stairs are extra. Here is the full breakdown, plus what wood asks of you after it is built.

## Pressure-treated vs cedar for a 12x20 deck

Both are real wood. They look, age and cost differently.

### Pressure-treated wood

Pressure-treated lumber is treated with preservatives to resist rot and insects. It is the lowest cost material to build with and is strong enough for any deck frame. Fresh boards have a green or tan tint and are usually stained once the wood has dried out.

### Cedar

Cedar is naturally resistant to decay, lighter in weight and has a warm colour and grain that many people love. It costs more than pressure-treated. Like any wood deck, it needs regular cleaning and staining to keep its colour. Left alone, it weathers to grey.

### Which one should you choose?

- **Pick pressure-treated** if budget comes first and you are fine staining every few years.
- **Pick cedar** if you want the look and feel of a premium wood deck.
- **Consider composite** if you want to skip staining altogether. Our guide to [composite deck cost per square foot](/post/composite-deck-cost-per-square-foot-ottawa) shows the numbers.

## Railings and stairs for a 12x20 wood deck

### Railing

If the 20 ft side is against the house, the open sides are 12 + 20 + 12 = 44 linear feet. With a 4 ft stair opening, that is about **40 linear feet** of railing.

- **Wood railing** ($60 to $80 per linear ft): about $2,400 to $3,200
- **Aluminum railing** ($90 to $140 per linear ft): about $3,600 to $5,600

Many homeowners pair a wood deck with black aluminum railing. It gives a modern look and the railing never needs staining. See styles on our [railings page](/railings).

Ontario's Building Code generally requires a guard (railing) when a deck's walking surface is more than 600 mm (24 in.) above the ground. A low 12x20 deck may not need one. Confirm your project with the City of Ottawa.

### Stairs

- **1 to 4 steps:** about $800 to $1,800
- **Full flight:** about $2,500 to $5,500

## The upkeep side of a wood deck

The build price is only part of the story with wood. Ottawa winters, spring thaws and summer sun are hard on any wood deck.

- **Clean it every spring** to remove mildew and grime.
- **Stain or seal it every few years** to protect the wood and keep the colour.
- **Check boards and fasteners** for cupping, cracks or loose screws, and fix them early.

You can do this yourself or have it done. We offer [deck staining](/staining) for wood decks across Ottawa. If you would rather skip it, composite never needs staining.

## What else changes the cost

- **Height.** Raised decks need taller posts, bigger beams, a railing and a full flight of stairs.
- **Permit.** In Ottawa, an attached deck more than 600 mm (24 in.) above the ground needs a building permit. Read our [deck permit guide](/post/deck-permit-ottawa).
- **Old deck removal.** Taking down an existing deck adds to the quote.
- **Extras.** Lighting, skirting and privacy screens are priced on their own.
